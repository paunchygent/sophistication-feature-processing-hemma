Hemma Research Workstation

HOME
  Hemma Home opens /home/paunchygent. Choose any research folder there.
  ELLIPSE, private-cohort and workshop buttons are optional conveniences.

TOOLS
  TAALED: choose a flat folder of lowercase .txt files, select indices and run.
  Its worker continues after its GUI closes or the browser disconnects.
  Reopen TAALED to view status; a container restart can interrupt that worker.

  TAALES: choose arbitrary paths on the route card, then copy them into the
  actual upstream application's input and output pickers. The card cannot
  choose for the binary or determine whether an analysis has completed.

  JASP: launch from the suite or desktop application menu. The official
  Flatpak runs on Hemma itself and displays in this same desktop. A launch
  acknowledgement is not proof that its window or analysis has opened.
  Use its ordinary Open/Save dialogs. Save before restarting the workstation.

RESULTS
  Save under /home/paunchygent or /srv/hemma-workstation/workspace.
  The latter is the existing durable service volume, not a second copy.
  Existing /hemma-home and /config/workspace aliases remain available in
  the container. The workspace and host home survive desktop-config resets.

  TAALED publishes a fresh completed folder only after checking its output.
  A .incomplete folder is an interrupted or failed run, not completed results.
  TAALES and JASP retain their own completion and save behavior.

DESKTOP
  LXQt supplies the panel, desktop and PCManFM-Qt. Openbox manages windows.
  Scott's portrait remains the desktop canary. No XFCE recovery loop is used.
  The Mac tunnel provides the workstation at http://127.0.0.1:13000/ and the
  Human-CJ staging preview at http://127.0.0.1:15174/.
  Browser reconnect is different from restarting the container/X server:
  the latter can terminate GUI applications and lose unsaved changes.
